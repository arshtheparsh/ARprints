Headphone Stand

A wall-mounted headphone stand with an extra hook for a keychain — or anything else.

Fusion 3603D printedWall-mounted

The problem — why I made it

I usually use my headphones when I'm at my desk, so I wanted a permanent spot to keep them instead of leaving them lying around.

Design process & key decisions

I took measurements of multiple headphones using a caliper, and measured their resting angle with a protractor. I averaged those values to design a stand that fits a wide variety of different-sized headphones.

What went wrong — and what I changed

  • At first my hook was a little too large, so it was conflicting with the headphone rest.
  • I used Fusion 360's scale tool to size the hook down and reposition it out of the way.
